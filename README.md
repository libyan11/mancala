# Mancala

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/mancala/)

https://libyan11.github.io/mancala/

Mancala (Kalah) in your browser, on phone or computer. Play online with a friend, or against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard. Works offline after the first visit. Part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

**Play with a friend:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. The other types the code and taps *Join room*.

**Rules**
- Each player has 6 pits with 4 seeds, and a store on their right.
- Pick one of your pits: its seeds are sown one by one, counter-clockwise, skipping the other player's store.
- Last seed in your own store: go again.
- Last seed in one of your empty pits: capture it and every seed in the pit opposite.
- When one side runs out, each player keeps the seeds left on their side. Most seeds wins.
